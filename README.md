SmartRent AI is a strategic solution designed to eliminate "economic blindness" in the rental market. Many renters make poor housing choices by focusing only on monthly rent while failing to account for fixed commitments, commute costs, and high deposits.

Our platform shifts the user’s role from a manual researcher to a Financial Curator by providing high-fidelity economic mock-ups instantly.

Problem Statement: Renters struggle to visualize their true "Real Cost of Living," leading to long-term financial instability.

Target Domain: Rapid visualization of urban housing and budget optimization.

Strategic Impact: Empowers fresh graduates and urban workers to protect their disposable income using AI-driven financial reasoning.
